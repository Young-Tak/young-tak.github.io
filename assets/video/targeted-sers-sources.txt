Identity in Light · Optics Edition

Young-Tak Kim, Ju Eun Cho, Min Ji Hwang, Shimayali Kaushal, Nitin Singhal, Jung Bin Kim, Synho Do and Dong-Kwon Lim. “Targeted Surface-Enhanced Raman Scattering for Highly Accurate Identification of Bacterial Species and Finding Spectral Signatures with Explainable Artificial Intelligence.” ACS Nano (2026). https://doi.org/10.1021/acsnano.6c00119

Study findings
The study included 14 microbial species, comprising 12 bacteria and two yeasts. Each experimental setting produced 17,500 spectra. The authors used 1,750 held-out spectra per setting for testing. With the targeted particles, the model reported 96.1% spectrum classification accuracy at 532 nm, 90.9% at 633 nm, and 76.9% at 785 nm.

An explainable AI analysis highlighted five influential Raman shifts for each species. The classification results come from the full spectral model. The study used cultured organisms and a spectrum-level split. Diagnostic performance in clinical specimens remains to be assessed.
