Seeing Beyond the Scan

Young-Tak Kim, So Hyeon Bak, Seon-Sook Han, Yunsik Son and Jinkyeong Park.
Non-Contrast CT-based Pulmonary Embolism Detection Using GAN-Generated Synthetic Contrast Enhancement: Development and Validation of an AI Framework.

The retrospective evaluation included 43 internal patients and 62 patients from another hospital. Patient-level models used five-fold stratified cross-validation within each cohort. The reported AUCs for real and synthetic contrast were 0.861 and 0.836 internally, and 0.787 and 0.680 in the external cohort. Small cohorts and wide confidence intervals limit interpretation.
