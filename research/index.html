Research story

Research overview

My research question

What should determine when and how we trust AI in medicine? A model can perform well on average and still be wrong for an individual patient. I study the evidence clinicians need before acting on its advice.

Research overview

Long-term goal

What stays with the clinician when the AI is switched off? My goal is safe AI for clinical practice that helps clinicians recognize errors and strengthen their independent clinical judgment over time.

Clinical AI safety

Current work

Where does a model become reliable enough to act on? I developed the Safety-Aware Receiver Operating Characteristic (SA-ROC) framework to distinguish rule-in, rule-out, and review zones. My next step is to test how those boundaries shift across patient cohorts.

Clinical AI safety

Next study

A clinician and an AI can agree on the same mistake. Building on our mammography reader study, I plan to test how large language models communicate uncertainty and whether independent review reveals errors clinicians and AI miss together.

Explainable biomedical AI

Current work

Which Raman peaks distinguish species? In our ACS Nano study, we traced bacterial classifications to features in the spectrum and organized those features into a barcode.

Explainable biomedical AI

Next experiment

Can a spectral signature predict what a new sample will show? I’m studying whether Raman spectra distinguish finer molecular differences, including DNA variants. An explanation should tell us which band to measure in an independent sample.

Biomedical knowledge graphs

Current work

Where did this microbe–disease link come from? I co-developed MINERVA, a large-scale microbiome knowledge graph. Researchers can follow an association back to the passage where the authors reported it.

Biomedical knowledge graphs

Research direction

Does the published evidence apply to this patient? I plan to match conditions in guidelines and studies against patient records. A missing result or conflicting finding should be visible before a clinician acts on a recommendation.
